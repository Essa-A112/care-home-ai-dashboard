# SHAP Analysis for Cambridge Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for the Cambridge Enriched Local Authority District is negatively impacted primarily due to high percentages of care quality ratings that require improvement and poor house price growth. These factors suggest challenges in care home quality and economic conditions that could deter investment.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ Major driver; negative growth in house prices indicates a declining property market, which adversely affects investment attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of care homes rated as good further detracts from the overall quality perception in the area.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over may increase demand for care services but also raises concerns about the adequacy of current care provisions.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; while a small percentage of homes rated inadequate does not significantly impact the score, it still reflects quality concerns.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per 10,000 people suggests potential oversupply or competition, negatively affecting investment viability.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings provides some positive quality assurance but is insufficient to offset negative factors.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may imply greater demand but can also strain existing resources, impacting care quality perceptions.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; a slight increase in gross disposable household income per head suggests some economic stability, but its impact is minimal.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement alongside poor house price growth indicates a challenging investment environment.
- While there are some positive indicators, such as a small percentage of outstanding ratings, they are not strong enough to counterbalance the major negative drivers.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the combination of quality concerns and economic indicators suggests careful evaluation before investment.
