# SHAP Analysis for Calderdale Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Calderdale Enriched is positively influenced primarily by house price growth, which is a major driver. However, the presence of several negative factors related to care quality ratings from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) significantly detracts from the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local property market, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Significant detractor; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement signals potential quality issues, negatively impacting investment appeal.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary detractor; a notable proportion of care homes rated as good suggests some quality concerns, which may affect overall confidence.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor positive; a small percentage of outstanding care homes contributes positively, indicating some high-quality options available.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor detractor; the presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about care standards, impacting the investment score.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Minor detractor; lower gross disposable household income per head suggests limited financial capacity among residents, which could affect care home viability.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor detractor; a larger population may indicate demand, but without corresponding quality, it could lead to oversupply issues.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Minor positive; a higher percentage of older residents may indicate a growing demand for care services, supporting investment potential.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate may deter potential investors despite the positive house price growth.
- The balance between population size and care home quality raises concerns about sustainability and demand for services.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while house price growth is promising, the quality issues in care services present significant risks that need to be addressed.
