# Feature interpretation guide for SHAP

**House_Price_Growth_%**
- Definition: Percentage change in average house prices over the period (2020–2024).
- Expected impact: higher_is_better
- Why it matters: Signals local market momentum and potential capital appreciation.
- Typical range: min -83.880, p25 -3.410, p50 6.160, p75 15.080, max 47.310
- Known SHAP aliases: House=Price Growth %, House Price Growth %, house_price_growth_%
- Unit: percentage points

**%_CQC_RequiresImprovement**
- Definition: Share of CQC-registered homes rated 'Requires Improvement'.
- Expected impact: lower_is_better
- Why it matters: High proportion indicates quality risks and remediation costs.
- Typical range: min 0.000, p25 11.725, p50 18.600, p75 24.125, max 100.000
- Known SHAP aliases: %=CQC RequiresImprovement, % CQC RequiresImprovement
- Unit: %

**%_CQC_Good**
- Definition: Share of CQC-registered homes rated 'Good'.
- Expected impact: higher_is_better
- Why it matters: Higher share suggests a healthier care environment and lower operational risk.
- Typical range: min 0.000, p25 70.885, p50 76.470, p75 83.395, max 96.670
- Known SHAP aliases: %=CQC Good, % CQC Good
- Unit: %

**%_CQC_Inadequate**
- Definition: Share of CQC-registered homes rated 'Inadequate'.
- Expected impact: lower_is_better
- Why it matters: Indicates severe quality shortcomings and potential regulatory pressure.
- Typical range: min 0.000, p25 0.000, p50 0.000, p75 1.925, max 18.180
- Known SHAP aliases: %=CQC Inadequate, % CQC Inadequate
- Unit: %

**%_CQC_Outstanding**
- Definition: Share of CQC-registered homes rated 'Outstanding'.
- Expected impact: higher_is_better

- Typical range: min 0.000, p25 0.000, p50 3.120, p75 6.250, max 25.000
- Known SHAP aliases: %=CQC Outstanding, % CQC Outstanding

**%_CQC_NotRated**
- Definition: Share of CQC-registered homes without a published rating.
- Expected impact: lower_is_better
- Why it matters: Unrated stock increases uncertainty around quality and compliance.
- Typical range: min 0.000, p25 0.000, p50 0.000, p75 0.000, max 0.000
- Known SHAP aliases: %=CQC NotRated, % CQC NotRated
- Unit: %

**GDHI_per_head_2022**
- Definition: Gross disposable household income per head (2022).
- Expected impact: higher_is_better
- Why it matters: Higher incomes support fee sustainability and private-pay demand.
- Typical range: min 15,075.000, p25 19,460.000, p50 22,236.000, p75 25,884.000, max 96,578.000
- Known SHAP aliases: GDHI=per head 2022, GDHI per head 2022, gdhi_per_head_2022
- Unit: £ per person

**Care_Homes_per_10k**
- Definition: Number of registered care homes per 10,000 residents.
- Expected impact: ambiguous
- Why it matters: Higher supply can mean choice but may also reflect competition; interpret with demand metrics.
- Typical range: min 0.304, p25 2.071, p50 2.654, p75 3.503, max 7.818
- Known SHAP aliases: Care=Homes per 10k, Care Homes per 10k, care_homes_per_10k
- Unit: homes per 10k

**Percent_65plus**
- Definition: Population aged 65+ as a share of total population.
- Expected impact: higher_is_better
- Why it matters: Larger elderly cohort typically raises demand for care places.
- Typical range: min 5.810, p25 16.925, p50 19.780, p75 23.120, max 34.180
- Known SHAP aliases: Percent=65plus, Percent 65plus, percent_65plus
- Unit: %

**Total_Population**
- Definition: Resident population (latest available year).
- Expected impact: higher_is_better
- Why it matters: Bigger markets can support a wider range of care provision.
- Typical range: min 2,229.000, p25 108,511.000, p50 146,655.000, p75 237,948.500, max 1,166,049.000
- Known SHAP aliases: Total=Population, Total Population, total_population
- Unit: people
