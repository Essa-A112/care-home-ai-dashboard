# Charnwood Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Charnwood Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and the proportion of care homes rated as requiring improvement. However, the presence of a high percentage of care homes rated as good negatively impacts the score, indicating potential quality concerns.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; significant growth in house prices suggests a strong local economy and potential for investment returns.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement indicates a need for investment to enhance service quality.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as good negatively impacts the score, suggesting that while some facilities are performing well, the overall quality may still be lacking.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes contributes positively, indicating some high-quality options available.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes highlights areas needing urgent attention and investment.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a decreasing population could indicate reduced demand for care services, negatively impacting the investment score.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which can be a positive factor for investment.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the ability of residents to afford care services, negatively affecting the score.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services, positively influencing the investment outlook.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may signal systemic issues that could deter investment despite the potential for returns.
- The negative impact of the percentage of care homes rated as good suggests that quality concerns may overshadow the positive aspects of demand.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are strong indicators for investment, quality concerns and demographic factors warrant careful consideration.
