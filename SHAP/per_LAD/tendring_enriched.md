## Risks or Caveats
- The predominance of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" indicates a potential quality issue, which may deter investment despite the demand from an aging population.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while demand is present, the quality ratings pose significant risks to investment viability.
