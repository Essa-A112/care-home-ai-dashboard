# Tandridge Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the Tandridge Enriched Local Authority District is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). This significant drawback is somewhat mitigated by the presence of homes rated as "Inadequate" and a growing elderly population, which could indicate potential demand for care services.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" significantly decreases the investment score.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Secondary driver; the presence of homes rated as "Inadequate" suggests serious quality concerns, but its positive contribution indicates some potential for improvement.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a growing elderly population (65+) indicates increased demand for care services, positively influencing the score.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher number of care homes per 10,000 people suggests better access to care services, enhancing the investment appeal.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ Minor driver; a percentage of homes rated as "Good" contributes positively, indicating some quality care options available.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests residents may afford better care services, positively impacting the score.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; the presence of "Outstanding" rated homes adds to the overall quality perception, positively affecting the investment score.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Minor driver; growth in house prices may indicate a desirable area, potentially attracting more residents and care demand.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" poses a significant risk to overall care quality, which could deter potential investors despite other positive indicators.
- The balance between the number of care homes and the quality ratings may not meet the expected demand, leading to potential oversupply issues.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for demand, the quality concerns highlighted by the CQC ratings require careful consideration before investment.
