# Chorley Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the Chorley Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and significant house price growth. However, the presence of a lower percentage of care homes rated as 'Good' negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant need for improvement in care quality, suggesting potential for investment.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy, which can support care home investments.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of homes rated 'Good' suggests quality concerns, which can deter investment.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing elderly population indicates increasing demand for care services.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated 'Outstanding' reflects some high-quality options, which can enhance the area's appeal.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of inadequate ratings indicates some facilities may require urgent attention, impacting overall perception.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a decrease in total population could signal reduced demand for care services.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income suggests residents have more financial capacity to afford care services.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita indicates better access to care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may indicate systemic issues in care quality, which could affect long-term investment viability.
- The contrast between strong house price growth and a lower percentage of 'Good' rated care homes suggests a potential mismatch between economic growth and care quality.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators, the quality of care homes requires careful consideration before investment.
