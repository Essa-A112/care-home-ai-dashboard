# SHAP Analysis for St Helens Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for St Helens Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth, which is a major driver of investment potential. However, the presence of a high percentage of care quality ratings that require improvement negatively impacts the overall score, indicating potential risks in care quality.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy and potential for increased investment returns.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care facilities rated as requiring improvement suggests quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care facilities rated as good indicates some quality but still reflects a risk due to the presence of lower ratings.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a growing elderly population suggests increasing demand for care services, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care facilities indicates some high-quality options, which can positively influence the perception of care in the area.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the financial capacity of residents to afford care services.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes relative to the population may indicate oversupply, which can lead to increased competition and pressure on pricing.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a stagnant or declining population can limit the growth potential for care services in the area.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care facilities requiring improvement poses a significant risk, as it may lead to reputational damage and reduced demand for care services.
- While house price growth is strong, the underlying quality issues in care could lead to future challenges in attracting residents or maintaining occupancy rates.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns in care services warrant a careful evaluation of investment potential.
