# Wandsworth Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Wandsworth Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). Additionally, demographic factors such as the percentage of the population aged 65 and over and house price growth further detract from the score.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ (major driver) - A significant portion of care homes in the area are rated as requiring improvement, which heavily lowers the investment score.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ (major driver) - The presence of care homes rated as good also negatively affects the score, indicating quality concerns.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ (secondary) - A higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services, but also raises concerns about service quality.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↓ (secondary) - Slower growth in house prices may indicate economic stagnation, impacting investment attractiveness.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↓ (secondary) - A lower number of care homes per 10,000 people suggests potential accessibility issues, which can deter investment.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ (minor) - A larger total population can indicate demand but may also strain existing care services if quality is lacking.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ (minor) - The presence of inadequate care homes further diminishes the overall quality perception in the area.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ (minor) - Higher gross disposable household income per head can positively influence investment, but its impact is limited compared to negative factors.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" raises significant quality concerns, which could deter potential investors despite demographic demand.
- Economic indicators such as house price growth and disposable income suggest a mixed outlook, complicating investment decisions.

Proceed with caution — the high percentage of care homes rated poorly poses a significant risk to investment viability.
