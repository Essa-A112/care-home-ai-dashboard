# Rossendale Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Rossendale Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement. However, the presence of care homes rated as inadequate and a lower percentage of homes rated as good negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests potential for operational enhancements and increased future demand.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of homes rated as good detracts from the overall quality perception, negatively impacting investment appeal.
- **Total Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing population supports demand for care services, contributing positively to the investment score.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita indicates better accessibility to care services, which is favorable for investment.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about quality and safety, negatively affecting the investment score.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the financial capacity of residents to afford care services, impacting demand.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services, positively influencing the score.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement may indicate underlying quality issues that could deter potential investors.
- The presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about the overall care environment, which could affect future demand and reputation.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are strong growth indicators, quality concerns in care homes may pose risks to investment stability.
