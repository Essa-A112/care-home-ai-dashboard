# Rutland Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Rutland Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and significant house price growth. However, the presence of care homes rated as good and inadequate negatively impacts the score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a strong positive impact, indicating that a significant portion of care homes in the area are rated as requiring improvement, which may suggest potential for enhancement and investment.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: Another major driver, reflecting robust growth in house prices, which can indicate a healthy local economy and increased demand for care services.
- **CQC Good (↓)**: This is a secondary driver negatively affecting the score, as a lower percentage of care homes rated as good suggests quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **Percent 65plus (↑)**: This factor positively influences the score, indicating a growing elderly population that may increase demand for care services.
- **Total Population (↑)**: A positive contributor, suggesting a larger overall population which can support a more extensive care market.
- **GDHI per head 2022 (↑)**: This factor positively impacts the score, reflecting higher disposable income per person, which can enhance the ability to pay for care services.
- **CQC Outstanding (↑)**: A minor positive driver, indicating some care homes are rated outstanding, which can enhance the overall perception of care quality in the area.
- **CQC Inadequate (↓)**: This negatively affects the score, as a presence of care homes rated inadequate raises concerns about the quality of care available.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement alongside inadequate ratings may signal quality issues that could affect long-term sustainability and reputation.
- While house price growth is strong, it may not directly correlate with care home performance or demand, necessitating careful market analysis.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the mixed quality ratings require thorough due diligence before investment.
