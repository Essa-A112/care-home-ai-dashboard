# SHAP Analysis for Sefton Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Sefton Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by house price growth, which is declining, and the quality ratings of care homes, with a significant proportion rated as inadequate. Conversely, the high percentage of the population aged 65 and over and the presence of care homes per capita are positive contributors to the score.

## Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ pushes score down; declining house prices negatively affect investment attractiveness.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ pushes score up; a higher percentage of older adults indicates greater demand for care services.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ pushes score down; a lower proportion of care homes rated as good diminishes confidence in care quality.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ pushes score up; more care homes per capita suggests better access to services for the elderly population.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ pushes score up; a higher percentage of outstanding-rated homes enhances the overall quality perception.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ pushes score down; a significant number of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care standards.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ pushes score down; a larger population may indicate higher demand but could also strain resources if not managed well.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ pushes score up; homes requiring improvement indicate potential for quality enhancement, though they are not ideal.

## Risks or Caveats
- The negative impact of inadequate care home ratings may overshadow the benefits of a growing elderly population.
- High demand for care services could lead to resource strain if quality does not improve concurrently.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the significant concerns regarding care quality and house price trends warrant careful consideration before investment.
