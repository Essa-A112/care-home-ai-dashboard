# SHAP Analysis for Worthing Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Worthing Enriched is positively influenced primarily by significant house price growth, which is a major driver of potential returns. However, the presence of negative indicators related to Care Quality Commission (CQC) ratings suggests potential risks in care quality that could impact long-term sustainability.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth indicates a robust property market, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of good ratings suggests quality concerns in care services, negatively impacting the score.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage indicates that many care homes are not meeting expected standards, further detracting from the score.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher proportion of older residents suggests increased demand for care services, positively influencing investment potential.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita indicates better access to services, which can enhance community support.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a significant percentage of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care quality, negatively affecting the score.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings indicates some high-quality options, which can enhance the overall perception of care in the area.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a slight negative impact as a larger population may strain resources if care quality is already low.
  
## Risks or Caveats
- The positive impact of house price growth may be undermined by the negative indicators related to care quality, suggesting a potential mismatch between demand and service quality.
- The presence of a significant percentage of care homes rated as inadequate could deter potential investors concerned about long-term viability.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns highlighted by CQC ratings warrant careful consideration before investment.
