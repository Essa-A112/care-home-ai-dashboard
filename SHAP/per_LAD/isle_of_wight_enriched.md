# SHAP Analysis for Isle Of Wight Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for the Isle Of Wight Enriched Local Authority District is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care services rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). While house price growth is a positive factor, the overall quality of care services presents a significant risk to investment attractiveness.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care services rated as "Requires Improvement" significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices indicates a potentially attractive market for investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care services rated as "Good" still contributes negatively to the overall score.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per 10,000 people indicates better service availability.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of care services rated as "Outstanding" adds positively to the score.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of services rated as "Inadequate" also contributes positively, though it is a concern.
- **Total=Population**: ↑ Minimal driver; a larger population may imply greater demand for care services, but the effect is negligible.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minimal driver; a slight decrease in gross disposable household income per head suggests economic pressures that could affect investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care services rated as "Requires Improvement" poses a significant risk to the overall attractiveness of the investment.
- While house price growth is positive, it may not be sustainable if care quality does not improve.
- The balance between demand (due to an aging population) and quality of care services is critical; poor quality could deter potential residents.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the investment landscape is tempered by quality concerns despite some positive economic indicators.
