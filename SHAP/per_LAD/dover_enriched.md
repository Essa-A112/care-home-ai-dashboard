# SHAP Analysis for Dover Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Dover Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and strong house price growth. However, the presence of care homes rated as good, inadequate, and outstanding negatively impacts the overall score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a significant positive impact, indicating a high percentage of care homes needing improvement, which suggests potential for investment in upgrades.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: A strong positive factor, reflecting an increasing property market that can enhance investment attractiveness in the area.
- **Percent 65+ (↑)**: This secondary driver indicates a growing elderly population, which typically increases demand for care services.
- **CQC Good (↓)**: This negatively impacts the score, as a notable percentage of care homes rated as good suggests that not all facilities meet higher standards, which may deter investment.
- **Care Homes per 10k (↑)**: A minor positive driver, indicating a reasonable availability of care homes relative to the population, which supports service access.
- **CQC Inadequate (↓)**: This factor negatively affects the score, as a percentage of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care quality.
- **GDHI per head 2022 (↑)**: A minor positive influence, reflecting the general disposable income level, which can support care service affordability.
- **CQC Outstanding (↓)**: This negatively impacts the score, as a low percentage of outstanding ratings suggests limited top-tier care options available.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement alongside significant inadequacies raises concerns about overall care quality, which could affect long-term investment viability.
- The mixed ratings from CQC indicate that while there are opportunities for improvement, there are also risks associated with investing in lower-quality facilities.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are promising growth indicators, the quality of care services presents significant risks that need to be addressed.
