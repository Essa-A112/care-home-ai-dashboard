# SHAP Analysis for Sheffield Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Sheffield Enriched is positively influenced primarily by significant house price growth, which is a major driver of potential returns. However, the presence of a high percentage of care quality ratings requiring improvement and good ratings negatively impacts the overall score, indicating potential quality concerns in care services.

- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of inadequate ratings highlights some areas of concern in care quality.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care services rated as requiring improvement could deter potential investors despite the positive house price growth.
- The mixed quality ratings (good and outstanding) suggest variability in care service standards, which may impact overall demand.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns in care services warrant careful consideration before investment.
