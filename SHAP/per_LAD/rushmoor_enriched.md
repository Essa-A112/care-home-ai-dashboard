# SHAP Analysis for Rushmoor Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Rushmoor Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care services rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). This significant detractor is compounded by the demographic of the population, particularly the percentage of residents aged 65 and over, which also negatively influences the score.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care services in this category significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices indicates a potentially attractive market for investment.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of older residents suggests increased demand for care services, but also raises concerns about service quality.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; a low percentage of outstanding care services detracts from the overall quality perception.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a limited number of good-rated services further impacts the investment attractiveness.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita may indicate oversupply or lower quality options.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate services negatively affects the overall care environment.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population size has a negligible effect on the score.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; a slight increase in gross disposable household income per head suggests some economic resilience.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care services rated as "Requires Improvement" poses a significant risk to investment viability.
- While house price growth is positive, it may not be sufficient to offset the concerns regarding care quality.
- The demographic trend of an aging population could lead to increased demand but also highlights potential service inadequacies.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the high percentage of care services rated as "Requires Improvement" raises significant concerns about the quality of care in the area.
