# SHAP Analysis for Colchester Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Colchester Enriched is positively influenced by the quality of care ratings, particularly the percentages of care homes requiring improvement and those rated inadequate. Additionally, house price growth and economic indicators contribute positively, although the presence of a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over slightly detracts from the score.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates significant quality concerns, negatively impacting the investment score.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Major driver; a notable percentage of inadequate ratings further emphasizes quality issues, adversely affecting the score.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices suggests a healthy property market, which can attract investment.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Secondary driver; higher gross disposable household income per head indicates greater economic capacity in the area, supporting investment potential.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Secondary driver; a reasonable number of care homes per capita suggests adequate supply, which is favorable for investment.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings indicates some high-quality care options, positively influencing the score.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ Minor detractor; a higher percentage of the elderly population may indicate increased demand for care services, but it also raises concerns about resource strain.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of good ratings contributes positively, though its impact is limited compared to the negative ratings.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentages of care homes rated as requiring improvement and inadequate suggest significant quality issues that could deter investment despite positive economic indicators.
- The presence of a larger elderly population may increase demand for care services, but it also raises concerns about the adequacy of current care provisions.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive economic indicators, the quality of care ratings presents significant risks that need to be addressed before making investment decisions.
