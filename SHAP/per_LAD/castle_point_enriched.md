# SHAP Analysis for Castle Point Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Castle Point Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and strong house price growth. However, the presence of homes rated "Inadequate" and "Good" negatively impacts the overall score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests a significant opportunity for investment in quality enhancement.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy, which can support care home investments.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **Total = Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing population can lead to increased demand for care facilities.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes indicates some high-quality options in the area.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor risk; the presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about quality and could deter investment.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Minor risk; a percentage of homes rated as good may not be sufficient to assure overall quality, impacting investor confidence.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests residents may have more financial capacity for care services.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; the number of care homes per 10,000 people indicates availability, which can support investment decisions.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of homes rated as "Inadequate" and "Good" may signal potential quality issues that could affect long-term investment viability.
- While house price growth is strong, it does not guarantee sustained demand for care services if quality remains inconsistent.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are strong growth indicators, the mixed quality ratings necessitate a careful evaluation of potential investments.
