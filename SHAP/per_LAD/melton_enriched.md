# Melton Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the Melton Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). However, the negative impact of house price growth suggests potential affordability issues that could affect future investments.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant need for improvement, which may attract investment for upgrades.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ Major driver; negative growth suggests declining property values, which could deter investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of homes rated "Good" reflects a positive quality of care, enhancing the district's attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of inadequate ratings indicates some facilities may require urgent attention, presenting both a risk and an opportunity for investment.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; while a small percentage, the presence of outstanding ratings enhances the overall perception of care quality.
- **Total=Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a larger population can indicate a broader market for care services.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; a positive figure suggests a reasonable level of disposable income, supporting potential investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may indicate systemic issues that could deter investment despite the potential for improvement.
- Negative house price growth could signal economic challenges in the area, impacting long-term investment viability.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the risks associated with property values and care quality ratings warrant careful consideration.
