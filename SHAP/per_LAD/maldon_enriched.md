# SHAP Analysis for Maldon Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Maldon Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth, which is a major driver of investment potential. However, the presence of a notable percentage of care quality ratings that require improvement poses a risk to the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy and potential for increased investment returns.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care facilities rated as requiring improvement negatively impacts the investment score, suggesting quality concerns.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Secondary driver; a smaller percentage of inadequate ratings indicates some facilities are performing poorly, which could deter investment.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests a growing demand for care services, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **Total Population**: ↑ Minor driver; an increasing population can indicate a larger market for care services, supporting investment potential.
- **% CQC Good**: ↑ Minor driver; a percentage of good ratings contributes positively, reflecting some quality care options available in the area.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings enhances the perception of care quality, positively influencing investment sentiment.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests residents have more financial capacity to afford care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The significant percentage of care facilities rated as requiring improvement may deter potential investors despite the positive indicators from house price growth and demographic trends.
- The balance between the number of good and inadequate ratings could create uncertainty regarding the overall quality of care in the area.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns highlighted by CQC ratings warrant careful consideration before making investment decisions.
