# SHAP Analysis for Wyre Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Wyre Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). While there are positive indicators such as house price growth and a significant elderly population, the overall quality of care facilities remains a critical concern.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes in this category significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a potentially attractive market for investment.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes further detracts from the investment appeal.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing elderly population indicates increasing demand for care services.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a lower percentage of homes rated as good negatively affects the overall quality perception.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a reasonable number of care homes per capita suggests availability of services.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding homes adds some positive quality indicators.
- **Total = Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a stable population size supports ongoing demand for care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" poses a significant risk to the investment's attractiveness, despite positive indicators like house price growth.
- The balance between demand (due to an aging population) and the quality of care services could lead to potential reputational risks.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive market indicators, the quality of care facilities is a significant concern that could impact long-term investment viability.
