# Three Rivers Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the Three Rivers Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a relatively high percentage of care quality ratings that are either good or outstanding. However, the presence of care homes per capita shows a negative impact, indicating potential oversupply concerns.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy and increasing property values.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes requiring improvement indicates areas for potential enhancement in care quality.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Secondary driver; the presence of inadequate ratings reflects challenges in care quality that may deter investment.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income suggests increased spending power among residents, positively impacting investment potential.
- **Total Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing population indicates a larger market for care services, supporting investment viability.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a percentage of outstanding care ratings enhances the attractiveness of the care sector in the area.
- **% CQC Good**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of good ratings contributes positively to the overall perception of care quality.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a negative impact suggests potential oversupply of care homes, which could lead to increased competition and lower returns.
- **Percent 65+**: ↓ Minor driver; a small percentage of older residents may indicate limited demand for care services in the future.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate may pose risks to overall care quality and investor confidence.
- The negative impact of care homes per capita suggests a potential oversupply, which could lead to market saturation and reduced profitability.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are strong positive indicators, the quality concerns and potential oversupply warrant careful consideration before investment.
