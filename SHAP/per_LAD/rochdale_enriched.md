# Rochdale Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Rochdale Enriched is positively influenced by several factors, particularly the percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and house price growth. However, the presence of care homes rated as "Inadequate" and a declining gross disposable household income per head are notable detractors.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates significant room for improvement in care quality, which can attract investment for upgrades.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, enhancing investment appeal.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a positive percentage of homes rated "Good" reflects a reasonable quality of care, supporting investment confidence.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor detractor; a small percentage of homes rated "Inadequate" signals potential risks in care quality, which could deter investment.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ Minor detractor; a higher percentage of elderly residents may indicate increased demand for care services, but also potential strain on resources.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated "Outstanding" enhances the overall perception of care quality, positively influencing investment.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↓ Minor detractor; a decrease in gross disposable household income suggests reduced financial capacity among residents, which could limit investment potential.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor detractor; a declining population may indicate reduced demand for care services, negatively impacting investment attractiveness.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated "Requires Improvement" suggests a need for significant investment, which may not yield immediate returns.
- The presence of "Inadequate" rated homes could pose reputational risks and affect overall market confidence.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are strong growth indicators, the quality concerns and economic factors warrant careful evaluation before investment.
