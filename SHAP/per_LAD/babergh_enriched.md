# SHAP Analysis for Babergh Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Babergh Enriched is positively influenced primarily by house price growth, while quality indicators from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) present significant challenges. The presence of a high percentage of older adults also contributes positively, but the overall quality ratings from CQC detract from the score.

- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; significant increase in property values indicates a strong local economy and demand for care homes.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major detractor; a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement negatively impacts perceived quality and attractiveness for investment.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary detractor; a notable portion of care homes rated as good suggests quality issues that could deter potential investors.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of residents aged 65 and over indicates a growing market for care services.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor positive; a small percentage of outstanding-rated care homes adds some confidence in quality.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor detractor; the presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about overall care standards.
- **Total Population**: ↑ Minor positive; a larger population base can support demand for care services.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor positive; higher disposable income per head suggests residents may afford better care options.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement and inadequate could signal systemic quality issues, potentially affecting long-term investment viability.
- While house price growth is strong, it may not be sustainable if quality concerns persist, leading to a potential decline in demand.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, significant quality concerns from CQC ratings warrant careful consideration before investment.
