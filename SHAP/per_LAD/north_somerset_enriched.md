# North Somerset Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for North Somerset Enriched is positively influenced primarily by strong house price growth, which significantly boosts the score. However, the presence of a high percentage of care quality ratings that require improvement negatively impacts the overall assessment.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy and potential for investment returns.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Significant negative impact; a high percentage of care facilities rated as requiring improvement suggests quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary negative driver; a notable portion of care facilities rated as good indicates some quality but still reflects a need for improvement overall.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Positive influence; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor positive driver; a small percentage of outstanding care facilities indicates some high-quality options available in the area.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor positive influence; a reasonable number of care homes per capita suggests accessibility to care services.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor negative impact; a larger total population may indicate strain on resources if care quality is not maintained.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor negative driver; a small percentage of inadequate ratings further highlights quality concerns in the care sector.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care facilities requiring improvement may overshadow the positive aspects of house price growth and demographic demand.
- The balance between the number of care homes and the quality ratings indicates potential strain on service delivery.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns in care services warrant a thorough assessment before investment.
