# North Devon Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for North Devon Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and the proportion of care homes requiring improvement. However, the presence of care homes rated as good, inadequate, and outstanding negatively impacts the score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

# Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement indicates potential for operational enhancements and investment opportunities.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a growing elderly population suggests increasing demand for care services, which is favorable for investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as good may indicate quality concerns, negatively impacting the investment score.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes raises red flags about service quality, detracting from the overall score.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; while a small percentage of outstanding homes is positive, it is outweighed by the negative ratings, thus impacting the score.
- **Total=Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a larger population can support demand for care services, contributing positively to the investment outlook.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable income per head suggests residents have more financial capacity to invest in care services.

# Risks or Caveats
- The presence of a significant number of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate may pose risks to service quality and reputation.
- The mixed ratings from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) could lead to fluctuating demand and investment stability.

# Action
Proceed with caution; while there are strong growth indicators, the quality of care services presents potential risks that need to be addressed.
