# Executive Brief
The investment score for South Kesteven is negatively impacted primarily by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and declining house price growth. These factors suggest challenges in care quality and economic conditions that may deter investment.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ pushes score down - This is a major driver indicating that a significant portion of care homes in the area are not meeting quality standards.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↓ pushes score down - The negative growth in house prices suggests a weakening local economy, which can deter potential investors.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ pushes score up - A higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services, positively influencing investment potential.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↓ pushes score down - The presence of care homes rated as "Outstanding" is limited, which negatively affects the overall perception of care quality.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ pushes score down - A notable percentage of care homes rated as "Inadequate" further diminishes the attractiveness of the investment landscape.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ pushes score down - The low proportion of homes rated as "Good" indicates a lack of quality care options, which is a deterrent for investment.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↓ pushes score down - A lower number of care homes per capita suggests limited availability of services, impacting the investment score negatively.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↓ pushes score down - Lower gross disposable household income per head indicates reduced financial capacity among residents, which can affect care home viability.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of older adults may indicate demand for care, but the poor quality ratings suggest that existing services may not meet this demand effectively.
- Economic indicators such as house price growth and disposable income may conflict with the growing need for care services, creating a challenging investment environment.

## Action
Proceed with caution — The negative quality indicators and economic conditions warrant a careful evaluation of potential investments in this area.
