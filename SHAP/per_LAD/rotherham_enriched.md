## Executive Brief
The investment score for Rotherham Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement. However, the presence of a substantial population in care homes rated as inadequate and good negatively impacts the overall score.

- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests a demand for better quality care, which could lead to investment opportunities.

- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Secondary driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the ability of residents to afford care services.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a percentage of care homes rated inadequate raises concerns about care quality and investment risk.

- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests a competitive market, which could attract investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may indicate systemic issues in care quality, which could deter potential investors.
- The presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about the overall reputation of the care sector in Rotherham, potentially impacting future demand.

Proceed with caution; while there are opportunities for investment, the quality concerns in care homes warrant careful consideration.
