# Elmbridge Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Elmbridge Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and significant house price growth. However, the presence of care quality ratings indicating inadequate and good services negatively impacts the overall score.

# Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant need for improvement in care quality, which can attract investment for upgrades.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, enhancing investment potential.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of good ratings indicates quality concerns that may deter investment.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Secondary driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests greater financial capacity for care services.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care quality, negatively impacting the score.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings indicates some high-quality care options, which can be a positive signal.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher percentage of older residents may increase demand for care services but also indicates a potentially strained system.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which can enhance investment attractiveness.

# Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may signal systemic issues that could deter potential investors despite the positive economic indicators.
- The presence of inadequate care ratings alongside good ratings creates a mixed quality perception, which could complicate investment decisions.

# Action
Proceed with caution; while economic indicators are strong, the quality of care services presents significant risks that need to be addressed.
