# New Forest Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the New Forest Enriched Local Authority District is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). While positive factors such as house price growth and an aging population contribute positively, the overall quality ratings of care homes present a significant risk.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a positive economic environment, enhancing investment appeal.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a lower percentage of care homes rated as "Good" detracts from the overall quality perception.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of care homes rated as "Inadequate" further diminishes the investment score.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per 10,000 population indicates better access to care services.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher gross disposable household income per head suggests greater financial capacity for care services.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger total population can indicate higher demand but also raises concerns about service quality if not managed properly.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" poses a significant risk to the overall investment attractiveness, despite positive economic indicators.
- The balance between demand (aging population) and quality (CQC ratings) may lead to potential challenges in service delivery.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive economic indicators, the quality of care homes presents a substantial risk that must be addressed.
