# SHAP Analysis for Arun Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Arun Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement. However, the presence of a notable percentage of care homes rated as good negatively impacts the overall score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, enhancing investment appeal.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes needing improvement indicates potential for investment in quality enhancements.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a significant portion of care homes rated as good detracts from the score, suggesting quality concerns in the sector.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a greater number of care homes per 10,000 people suggests better access to care facilities.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger total population may indicate strain on resources, negatively impacting care quality.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a small percentage of inadequate care homes further highlights quality issues in the area.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests greater financial capacity for care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement alongside a significant number rated as good indicates a mixed quality landscape, which could affect long-term investment viability.
- The overall population growth may lead to increased demand for care services, but it could also strain existing resources if not managed effectively.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the mixed quality of care services presents potential risks that should be carefully evaluated.
