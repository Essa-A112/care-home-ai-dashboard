# Richmond Upon Thames Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Richmond Upon Thames Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). These factors indicate quality concerns that outweigh the positive influences of house price growth and demographic factors.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" significantly decreases the investment score.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as "Good" also contributes negatively, suggesting quality issues.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Positive driver; strong house price growth indicates a potentially attractive market for investment.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated "Outstanding" provides some positive influence on the score.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests better economic conditions for potential residents.
- **Percent = 65 plus**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over may indicate increased demand for care services but also raises quality expectations.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger total population can imply greater demand but may also strain existing care services if quality is lacking.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated "Inadequate" adds to the overall quality concerns.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" indicates significant quality issues that could deter potential residents and investors.
- While house price growth is positive, it may not compensate for the negative perceptions surrounding care quality in the area.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the quality concerns highlighted by the CQC ratings may pose risks to investment viability despite some positive economic indicators.
