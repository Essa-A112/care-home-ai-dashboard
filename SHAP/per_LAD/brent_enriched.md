# Executive Brief
The investment score for the Brent Enriched Local Authority District is negatively impacted primarily by declining house price growth and a high percentage of care quality ratings that require improvement. These factors indicate potential risks in the care home investment landscape within this district.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement and inadequate suggests significant quality issues that could deter potential investors.
- While the presence of outstanding care homes offers some positive outlook, it is not enough to mitigate the overall negative trends in quality ratings.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the negative indicators regarding care quality and house price growth warrant careful consideration before making investment decisions.
