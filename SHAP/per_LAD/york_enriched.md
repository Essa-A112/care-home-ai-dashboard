# SHAP Analysis for York Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for the York Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement. However, the presence of care homes rated as inadequate and a lower percentage of homes rated as good negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; significant growth in house prices indicates a strong local economy and potential for investment returns.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests a need for investment to enhance quality and services.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of homes rated as good indicates quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Secondary driver; the presence of inadequate care homes raises red flags regarding service quality and operational risks.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; a positive contribution from gross disposable household income per head suggests residents have more financial capacity, supporting care home viability.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita may indicate oversupply or competition, which could pressure profitability.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing elderly population increases demand for care services, positively impacting investment potential.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding-rated homes indicates some high-quality options, enhancing the attractiveness of the area.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and the presence of inadequate-rated homes may signal underlying quality issues that could affect long-term sustainability.
- The balance between supply (number of care homes) and demand (elderly population) needs careful monitoring to avoid market saturation.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, significant quality concerns in care homes warrant a thorough assessment before investment.
