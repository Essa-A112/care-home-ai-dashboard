# County Durham Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for County Durham Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by significant declines in house price growth and a high percentage of care quality ratings requiring improvement. These factors suggest a challenging environment for care-home investments in this Local Authority District.

## Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ Major driver; a significant decline in house price growth negatively affects investment attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement indicates potential quality concerns.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Secondary driver; a slight decrease in total population may suggest reduced demand for care services.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a small increase in the percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates a growing demographic that may require care services.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a low percentage of care homes rated as good reflects quality issues that could deter investment.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; a very small percentage of outstanding ratings further emphasizes quality concerns in the care sector.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; a slight decrease in gross disposable household income per head suggests limited financial capacity for care services.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a small percentage of inadequate ratings indicates some care homes are performing poorly.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of a growing elderly population may not compensate for the negative impacts of poor care quality ratings and declining house prices.
- The overall demand for care services could be undermined by the financial constraints indicated by GDHI trends.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the combination of declining house prices and care quality ratings presents significant risks to investment viability.
