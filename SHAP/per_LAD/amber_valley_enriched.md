# SHAP Analysis for Amber Valley Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Amber Valley Enriched is positively influenced primarily by significant house price growth, which is a major driver of potential returns. However, the presence of multiple quality indicators from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) that are rated as requiring improvement or inadequate negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust market, enhancing investment potential.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Significant negative impact; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests quality concerns.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary negative factor; a lower percentage of homes rated as good further detracts from the investment appeal.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Positive influence; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor negative impact; the presence of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care quality.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor negative influence; a larger population may indicate demand but also potential strain on services.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor positive factor; a small percentage of outstanding ratings provides some reassurance about care quality.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Minor negative impact; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit spending on care services.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor positive influence; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate may deter potential investors despite the positive house price growth.
- The balance between an aging population and care quality ratings raises concerns about sustainability and service delivery.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while house price growth is promising, the quality issues highlighted by CQC ratings warrant careful consideration before investment.
