# SHAP Analysis for East Suffolk Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for East Suffolk Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). This significant drawback is somewhat offset by positive indicators such as house price growth and an increasing elderly population.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes in this category significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices indicates a potentially attractive investment environment.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a growing elderly population suggests increasing demand for care services.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; a low percentage of outstanding care homes negatively affects the overall quality perception.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes further diminishes the investment appeal.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a limited number of good-rated homes contributes to the overall quality concerns.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may not necessarily correlate with better care home quality.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita can indicate better access to services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" poses a significant risk to investment viability.
- While house price growth is positive, it may not fully compensate for the quality issues in care services.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the negative quality indicators may outweigh the positive demographic and economic factors.
