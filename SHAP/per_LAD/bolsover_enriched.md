# Bolsover Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Bolsover Enriched is positively influenced primarily by significant house price growth, which is a major driver for potential returns. However, the presence of a high percentage of care quality ratings that require improvement negatively impacts the score, indicating potential risks in care quality.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; significant growth in house prices suggests a strong market potential for investment.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care facilities rated as requiring improvement indicates quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of facilities rated as good suggests limited quality assurance in care services.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing elderly population can increase demand for care services, positively influencing investment potential.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care facilities indicates some high-quality options, which can enhance the attractiveness of the area.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care facilities raises concerns about overall care quality.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the ability of residents to afford care services.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a reasonable number of care homes per capita suggests availability of services, which can be a positive factor for investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care facilities requiring improvement may lead to reputational risks and affect long-term sustainability.
- The balance between house price growth and care quality ratings indicates a potential mismatch between demand and service quality.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while house price growth is promising, the quality of care services presents significant risks that must be addressed.
