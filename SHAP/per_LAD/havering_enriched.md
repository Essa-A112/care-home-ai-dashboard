# SHAP Analysis for Havering Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Havering Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). These factors suggest quality concerns that outweigh the positive influences of house price growth and the presence of outstanding care ratings.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage indicates significant quality issues in care homes, negatively affecting the investment score.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; while a good rating is positive, the high percentage here still reflects a lack of excellence in care quality.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Positive driver; growth in house prices suggests a potentially attractive investment environment.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a small percentage of inadequate ratings further detracts from the overall quality perception.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor positive driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings provides some reassurance about care quality.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher percentage of older residents may indicate increased demand for care services, but also raises concerns about service adequacy.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may imply greater demand, but it does not directly correlate with care quality.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor positive driver; higher gross disposable income per head can indicate better financial capacity for care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" suggests significant quality concerns that may deter investment despite positive economic indicators.
- The balance between population growth and care quality ratings indicates potential demand but raises questions about service adequacy.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive economic indicators, the significant quality concerns in care homes warrant careful consideration before investment.
