# SHAP Analysis for Horsham Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Horsham Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). While there are some positive indicators such as house price growth and an aging population, the overall quality of care facilities is a significant concern.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; this high percentage indicates a significant quality issue in care homes, negatively affecting the investment score.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Secondary driver; a smaller but still notable percentage of care homes rated inadequate suggests ongoing quality challenges.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Positive driver; growth in house prices indicates a potentially attractive investment environment.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Positive driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Positive driver; higher gross disposable household income per head indicates greater financial capacity for care services.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; a low percentage of outstanding ratings reflects limited high-quality care options.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; the number of care homes per capita suggests availability, though it does not directly indicate quality.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated good contributes positively but is overshadowed by the negative ratings.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" poses a significant risk to the overall attractiveness of the investment.
- While demographic trends and economic indicators are positive, they may not compensate for the quality issues in care provision.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the investment environment shows potential, but significant quality concerns in care homes must be addressed.
