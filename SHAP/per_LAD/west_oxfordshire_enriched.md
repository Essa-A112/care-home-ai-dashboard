# SHAP Analysis for West Oxfordshire Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for West Oxfordshire Enriched is positively influenced primarily by significant house price growth, which is a major driver of investment potential. However, the presence of several negative factors related to care quality ratings from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) indicates potential risks that could impact future performance.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust property market, enhancing investment appeal.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Significant negative impact; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement raises concerns about care quality.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary negative factor; a notable portion of care homes rated as 'Good' indicates quality issues that could deter investment.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Positive influence; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor negative factor; the presence of inadequate care homes further diminishes the attractiveness of the investment landscape.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor positive factor; higher gross disposable household income per head indicates greater financial capacity for care services.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor negative factor; a larger population may imply increased demand, but it also raises concerns about resource allocation and care quality.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Very minor positive factor; a small percentage of outstanding care homes adds a slight positive note to the overall care quality landscape.

## Risks or Caveats
- The conflicting signals of high house price growth alongside multiple negative CQC ratings may indicate a potential mismatch between demand and quality of care services.
- The overall care quality ratings could impact long-term sustainability and attractiveness of the investment, despite current market conditions.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns highlighted by CQC ratings necessitate careful consideration before investment.
