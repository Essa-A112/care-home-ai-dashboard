# Executive Brief
The investment score for North Lincolnshire Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and the growth in house prices. However, the presence of care homes rated as "Outstanding" and the general economic indicators such as Gross Disposable Household Income (GDHI) per head are negatively impacting the score.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may indicate a potential for future improvements, but it also raises concerns about current care quality.
- The negative impact of GDHI suggests that while there may be demand due to an aging population, financial constraints could limit access to care services.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the quality concerns and economic factors warrant careful consideration.
