# SHAP Analysis for North East Lincolnshire Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for North East Lincolnshire Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). While there is positive growth in house prices and a significant elderly population, these factors are outweighed by the quality concerns in care provision.

- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes in this category significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices suggests a potentially attractive investment environment.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Secondary driver; a low percentage of outstanding care homes negatively affects the overall quality perception.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a significant elderly population indicates a strong demand for care services.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes further detracts from the investment appeal.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the financial capacity of potential clients.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a low percentage of good-rated homes contributes to quality concerns.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a reasonable number of care homes per capita suggests availability of services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" poses a significant risk to investment viability despite positive housing market indicators.
- The balance between an aging population and the quality of care services may lead to increased demand without sufficient supply of high-quality care.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators, the quality of care remains a critical concern that could impact long-term investment returns.
