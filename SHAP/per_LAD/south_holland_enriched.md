# SHAP Analysis for South Holland Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for South Holland Enriched is positively influenced by a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and strong house price growth. However, the presence of inadequate care quality ratings presents a potential risk to overall investment attractiveness.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (%)**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates significant room for improvement in care quality, which can attract investment for upgrades.
- **House Price Growth (%)**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth suggests a healthy property market, enhancing the area's attractiveness for investment.
- **Population Aged 65+ (%)**: ↑ Secondary driver; a growing elderly population indicates increasing demand for care services, supporting investment potential.
- **CQC Inadequate (%)**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate ratings signals quality concerns, which could deter investment.
- **Total Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a larger population may indicate a broader market for care services, positively influencing investment.
- **CQC Outstanding (%)**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings reflects some high-quality care options, which can enhance the area's reputation.
- **CQC Good (%)**: ↑ Minor driver; a positive rating contributes to overall care quality perception, supporting investment interest.
- **GDHI per Head (2022)**: ↓ Minor driver; a negative impact suggests lower disposable income, which may limit the ability of residents to afford care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement alongside inadequate ratings raises concerns about the quality of care, which could affect long-term investment sustainability.
- While house price growth is promising, it may not fully offset the risks associated with care quality issues.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators for investment, the quality concerns must be addressed to ensure sustainable returns.
