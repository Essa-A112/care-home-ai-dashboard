# SHAP Analysis for Sunderland Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Sunderland Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to significant declines in house price growth and a high percentage of care quality ratings requiring improvement. These factors suggest a challenging environment for care home investments in this Local Authority District.

- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ Major driver; a decline in house price growth negatively affects the investment score, indicating potential economic instability.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement suggests quality concerns that deter investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower proportion of care homes rated as good further diminishes confidence in the local care sector.

- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a declining total population can signal reduced demand for care services, negatively impacting the investment outlook.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which could support investment.

- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the ability of residents to afford care services, negatively affecting investment potential.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement alongside low ratings for good and outstanding care indicates significant quality issues that could deter potential investors.
- The demographic trend of an increasing elderly population may not be sufficient to offset the negative impacts of poor care quality and economic indicators.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are some positive demographic indicators, the significant quality concerns and economic challenges present substantial risks for investment.
