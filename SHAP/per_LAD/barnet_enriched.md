# SHAP Analysis for Barnet Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Barnet Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and significant house price growth. However, the presence of a notable percentage of care homes rated as good and a growing elderly population negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a high positive impact, indicating a significant number of care homes that need enhancements.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: A strong positive influence, suggesting that rising property values in the area may attract more investment.
- **CQC Good (↓)**: This is a secondary driver negatively affecting the score, as a lower percentage of homes rated good may indicate quality concerns.
- **Percentage of Population Aged 65+ (↓)**: This factor negatively impacts the score, reflecting potential challenges in meeting the needs of an aging demographic.
- **GDHI per Head 2022 (↑)**: A positive contributor, indicating higher disposable income levels which can support care home investments.
- **Total Population (↓)**: This factor negatively influences the score, suggesting a potential decline in demand for care services.
- **CQC Inadequate (↓)**: A minor negative driver, indicating some homes are rated inadequate, which can deter investment.
- **CQC Outstanding (↑)**: A small positive influence, reflecting a few homes that are rated outstanding, which can enhance the area's reputation.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may indicate systemic issues that could affect long-term investment viability.
- The negative impact of the aging population and total population metrics suggests potential demand challenges for care services.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns and demographic challenges warrant careful consideration before investment.
