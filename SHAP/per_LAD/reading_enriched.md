# SHAP Analysis for Reading Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Reading Enriched is primarily influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement," which significantly detracts from the overall score. Additionally, while house price growth is a positive factor, the aging population and other quality ratings negatively impact the investment outlook.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage indicates poor care quality, significantly lowering the score.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth suggests a favorable economic environment for investment.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of elderly residents may indicate increased demand for care, but also raises concerns about service adequacy.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a declining population can signal reduced demand for care services, negatively affecting the score.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; a low percentage of outstanding ratings reflects poor quality, further diminishing the investment score.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher number of homes per capita may indicate oversupply or competition, negatively impacting the score.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a low percentage of good ratings suggests quality issues, which detract from the investment appeal.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a presence of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care standards, adversely affecting the score.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head supports economic stability and potential investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" poses a significant risk to investment viability.
- While house price growth is positive, it may not compensate for the quality issues present in care services.
- The aging population could increase demand but also highlights the need for improved care standards.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the significant quality concerns in care services may outweigh the positive economic indicators.
