# SHAP Analysis for Torbay Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Torbay Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of residents aged 65 and over. However, the presence of care quality ratings indicating areas of concern, particularly in the "Good" and "Inadequate" categories, poses a risk to the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy and potential for investment returns.
- **Percent 65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of older residents suggests increased demand for care services.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Negative impact; a lower rating in this category indicates quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, supporting investment.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Minor driver; the presence of homes needing improvement indicates potential for growth but also raises quality concerns.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Negative impact; a significant number of inadequate ratings could harm the reputation and desirability of the area for care investment.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings reflects positively on the quality of care available.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Minor impact; a decrease in gross disposable household income per head may limit residents' ability to afford care services.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor impact; a declining or stagnant population could indicate reduced demand for care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of both high demand indicators (e.g., aging population) and quality concerns (e.g., inadequate care ratings) creates a mixed signal for potential investors.
- The balance between the number of care homes and their quality ratings suggests that while access is good, the quality of care may not meet expectations.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the quality concerns in care services must be addressed to ensure sustainable growth.
