# Executive Brief

These quality ratings suggest significant concerns regarding care standards, which adversely affect the attractiveness of investment in this Local Authority District.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" presents a significant risk, as it suggests systemic quality issues that could deter potential residents and investors.
