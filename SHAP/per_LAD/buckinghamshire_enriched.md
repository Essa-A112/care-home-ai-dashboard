# Buckinghamshire Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Buckinghamshire Enriched is positively influenced by a significant proportion of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). However, the presence of a large population and a notable percentage of older residents slightly detracts from the overall score.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant number of homes needing improvement, which could signal potential for future investment.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a considerable portion of homes rated as "Good" suggests a stable quality of care, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Minor driver; positive growth in house prices indicates a potentially lucrative market for care home investments.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests residents may afford better care options, supporting investment viability.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated "Outstanding" reflects high-quality care, positively influencing the investment score.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; while a small percentage, the presence of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care quality, which could affect investment decisions.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor detractor; a large population may indicate higher demand but also suggests potential strain on resources and services.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ Minor detractor; a higher percentage of older residents may increase demand for care but also highlights potential challenges in meeting care needs.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor detractor; a lower number of care homes per capita may indicate limited availability, which could impact service delivery.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may indicate systemic issues within the care sector that could affect long-term investment stability.
- The balance between a growing population and the quality of care could lead to increased pressure on existing services, potentially impacting future performance.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the significant number of homes requiring improvement necessitates careful evaluation of potential investments.
