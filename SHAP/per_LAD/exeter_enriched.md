## Executive Brief
The investment score for Exeter Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to poor Care Quality Commission (CQC) ratings, particularly the high percentage of care homes requiring improvement. This trend is compounded by stagnant house price growth, which further diminishes the attractiveness of investment in this area.

- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ Major driver; negative growth in house prices indicates a lack of economic vitality, discouraging investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of older adults may indicate a growing demand for care, which could be a positive signal if quality improves.
- The presence of both inadequate and requiring improvement ratings suggests systemic issues within the care sector that need addressing.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the negative indicators regarding care quality and economic conditions warrant a careful evaluation before making investment decisions.
