# SHAP Analysis for Forest Of Dean Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for the Forest Of Dean Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by significant house price growth, which is a major driver. However, the presence of a high percentage of care quality ratings requiring improvement and good ratings negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; significant growth in house prices indicates a strong property market, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement suggests quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable portion of care homes rated as good indicates some quality but still reflects potential issues in the sector.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services, positively influencing investment.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a greater number of care homes per 10,000 people indicates availability of services, supporting investment potential.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes reflects some high-quality options, which can enhance the overall perception of care in the area.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about care quality, negatively impacting investment sentiment.
- **Total Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a larger population base can indicate a broader market for care services, supporting investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The conflicting signals of high house price growth alongside a significant percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement may indicate underlying quality issues that could affect long-term sustainability.
- The presence of inadequate care homes could further exacerbate concerns about the overall quality of care in the area, potentially impacting future demand.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns in care services warrant careful consideration before investment.
