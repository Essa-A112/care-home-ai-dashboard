# SHAP Analysis for Mid Suffolk Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Mid Suffolk Enriched is positively influenced by several key factors, particularly the percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and house price growth. - **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates significant quality issues in care homes, pushing the score up.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, enhancing investment appeal.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of homes rated as "Good" supports a positive perception of care quality.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Secondary driver; the presence of inadequate ratings indicates areas needing improvement, which can attract investment for upgrades.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of older residents suggests increased demand for care services, positively impacting the investment score.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of homes rated as "Outstanding" can indicate a competitive market, but also highlights variability in care quality.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a lower number of homes per capita may suggest limited availability, which can deter investment.
- **Total = Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing population can indicate a larger market for care services, supporting investment potential.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may deter potential investors concerned about quality.
- The presence of homes rated as "Outstanding" alongside those rated poorly suggests a mixed quality landscape, which could complicate investment decisions.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are strong growth indicators, quality concerns must be addressed to ensure sustainable investment returns.
