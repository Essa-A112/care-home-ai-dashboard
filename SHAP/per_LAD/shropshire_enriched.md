The investment score for Shropshire Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as 'Requires Improvement' and strong house price growth. - **Percent 65+ (↑)**: This secondary driver reflects a growing elderly population, which typically increases demand for care services.

- **CQC Outstanding (↑)**: This factor positively contributes to the score, reflecting a small number of top-rated homes that enhance the overall perception of care quality.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of homes rated as 'Inadequate' poses a risk to the investment score, indicating potential quality issues that could deter prospective residents.
- The mixed ratings from CQC suggest that while there are opportunities for investment, there are also significant quality concerns that need to be addressed.
