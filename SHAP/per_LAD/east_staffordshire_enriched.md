# East Staffordshire Enriched SHAP Analysis

## Executive Brief
The investment score for East Staffordshire Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to declining house price growth and a significant proportion of care services requiring improvement. These factors suggest challenges in both property value stability and care quality, which are critical for attracting investment.

- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ pushes score down; a major driver indicating that declining house prices negatively affect investment attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ pushes score down; a significant factor showing that a high percentage of care services are rated as requiring improvement, raising concerns about quality.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ pushes score down; a secondary factor indicating that the proportion of care services rated as good is low, further detracting from the investment appeal.

## Risks or Caveats
- The combination of high care services requiring improvement and low house price growth presents a conflicting signal regarding the area's attractiveness for investment.
- While there are some positive indicators, such as the availability of care homes and a growing elderly population, they may not be sufficient to offset the negative impacts from quality ratings.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the negative drivers indicate significant risks that could affect the sustainability of investment returns.
