# SHAP Analysis for Luton Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Luton Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). However, the presence of a significant elderly population and the number of care homes per capita negatively impact the score, indicating potential challenges in meeting demand.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a need for improvement, which may suggest potential for investment in upgrades.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable portion of care homes rated as "Good" reflects a positive quality of care, enhancing attractiveness for investment.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; growth in house prices suggests a potentially increasing local economy, which can support care home investments.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ Negative impact; a high percentage of the population aged 65 and over may indicate increased demand for care services, but also potential strain on resources.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor negative impact; the presence of care homes rated as "Inadequate" raises concerns about quality and may deter investment.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor negative impact; a larger population may imply greater demand but could also indicate resource challenges if care home capacity is insufficient.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor negative impact; a low number of care homes relative to the population can lead to increased pressure on existing facilities.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor negative impact; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the ability of residents to afford care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" suggests a need for significant investment, which may not guarantee immediate returns.
- The balance between an aging population and the number of care homes could lead to increased competition and pressure on service quality.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the challenges related to quality and capacity must be carefully evaluated.
