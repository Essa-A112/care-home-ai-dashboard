# SHAP Analysis for Worcester Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Worcester Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to significant declines in house price growth and the quality ratings from the Care Quality Commission (CQC). These factors suggest a challenging environment for care home investments in this Local Authority District.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↓ pushes score down. A major negative driver, indicating a decline in property values which can deter investment.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ pushes score down. This significant factor suggests a high proportion of care homes are not meeting required standards, negatively impacting the investment appeal.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ pushes score down. A secondary driver, reflecting that a lower percentage of care homes are rated as good, further diminishing confidence in the sector.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ pushes score down. This factor indicates that some care homes are rated inadequate, which is detrimental to the overall investment score.
- **Percent 65+**: ↓ pushes score down. A minor driver, as a higher percentage of older adults may indicate increased demand but is offset by quality concerns.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↓ pushes score down. This factor suggests a lower availability of care homes relative to the population, which could indicate supply issues.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ pushes score down. A minor negative influence, as lower gross disposable household income may limit the ability of residents to afford care services.
- **Total Population**: ↑ pushes score up. A minor positive driver, indicating a larger population may suggest potential demand for care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate presents a significant risk to investment viability.
- While the total population is increasing, the quality of care homes is declining, which could lead to future demand issues.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the negative quality indicators and house price trends suggest potential challenges in the investment landscape.
