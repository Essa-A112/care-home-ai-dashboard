# SHAP Analysis for Stroud Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Stroud Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a relatively high percentage of older residents. However, the presence of lower ratings from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) negatively impacts the overall score, indicating potential quality concerns in care services.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust property market, enhancing investment appeal.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Significant negative impact; a lower percentage of care services rated as 'Good' raises concerns about quality and reliability.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of older residents indicates a growing demand for care services, which can support investment.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Secondary negative impact; a notable portion of services requiring improvement suggests potential risks in care quality.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor positive influence; having some services rated as 'Outstanding' provides a counterbalance to the negative CQC ratings.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor negative impact; the presence of inadequate services further detracts from the overall care quality perception.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor positive influence; a higher gross disposable household income per head indicates better financial capacity among residents, supporting care investment.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor negative impact; a larger population with a negative influence suggests potential strain on care resources.

## Risks or Caveats
- The conflicting signals of high house price growth alongside poor CQC ratings may indicate a market that is attractive but potentially risky due to quality concerns in care services.
- The percentage of older residents is promising for demand, but the quality of care services must be addressed to ensure sustainable investment.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns highlighted by CQC ratings necessitate careful evaluation before investment.
