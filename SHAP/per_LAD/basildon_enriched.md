# Basildon Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Basildon Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and a significant presence of homes rated "Inadequate." These factors outweigh the positive influence of the Gross Disposable Household Income per head, leading to an overall decrease in the investment attractiveness of this Local Authority District.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of homes in this category indicates quality concerns, negatively impacting the score.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a low percentage of homes rated "Good" suggests limited quality options for potential residents.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ Secondary driver; a higher number of homes per capita correlates with increased competition and potential oversupply, which can depress investment attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Secondary driver; a low percentage of homes rated "Outstanding" reflects a lack of top-quality care facilities in the area.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the elderly population may increase demand but also highlights the need for quality care, which is currently lacking.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Major driver; the presence of homes rated "Inadequate" raises significant concerns about care quality and safety.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; a positive indicator of disposable income may suggest some financial capacity for care, but its impact is limited compared to quality concerns.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may indicate demand, but without quality care options, this does not translate into a positive investment score.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" poses a significant risk to investment viability, overshadowing any potential demand from the elderly population.
- While Gross Disposable Household Income is a positive factor, it may not be sufficient to offset the quality issues present in the care homes.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the significant quality concerns in care homes may deter potential investment despite some positive economic indicators.
