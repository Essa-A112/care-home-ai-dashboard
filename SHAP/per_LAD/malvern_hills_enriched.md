# SHAP Analysis for Malvern Hills Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Malvern Hills Enriched is positively influenced by several key factors, particularly the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and strong house price growth. However, the presence of homes rated as "Inadequate" and "Good" introduces some complexity to the overall assessment.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a significant positive impact on the score, indicating a high percentage of care homes needing improvement, which may suggest potential for investment and growth.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: A strong secondary driver, reflecting an upward trend in property values, which can enhance the attractiveness of the area for investment.
- **Percent 65+ (↑)**: This factor indicates a growing elderly population, which is essential for demand in care services, contributing positively to the score.
- **Total Population (↑)**: A slight positive influence, suggesting a stable or growing community that supports care home viability.
- **CQC Good (↓)**: This factor negatively impacts the score, indicating that a portion of care homes are rated as "Good," which may limit the potential for improvement and investment opportunities.
- **GDHI per head 2022 (↑)**: A small positive contributor, reflecting the economic capacity of residents, which can support care home services.
- **CQC Inadequate (↓)**: This negatively affects the score, as a notable percentage of homes rated as "Inadequate" raises concerns about quality and investment risk.
- **Care Homes per 10k (↑)**: A minor positive driver, indicating the availability of care homes relative to the population, which can support service accessibility.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of care homes rated as "Inadequate" alongside those rated as "Requires Improvement" suggests potential quality issues that could deter investment.
- The mixed ratings from CQC (both "Good" and "Inadequate") may indicate variability in service quality, which could impact long-term sustainability.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the quality concerns highlighted by the CQC ratings warrant careful consideration.
