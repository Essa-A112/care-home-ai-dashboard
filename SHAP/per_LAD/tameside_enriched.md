# Tameside Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Tameside Enriched is positively influenced by a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and strong house price growth. However, the negative impact of total population and gross disposable household income per head suggests potential challenges in demand and financial viability.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant need for improvement in care quality, which may attract investment for upgrades.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, potentially increasing demand for care services.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as "Good" reflects a positive quality of care, enhancing the attractiveness of the area.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; while a small percentage indicates some facilities are underperforming, it may signal areas for improvement.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a very small percentage of outstanding ratings suggests limited top-tier care options, which could be a future investment opportunity.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a declining population could indicate reduced demand for care services, negatively impacting investment potential.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower disposable income per head may limit residents' ability to afford care services, posing a risk to financial viability.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a lower ratio of care homes may suggest insufficient supply to meet demand, potentially limiting growth opportunities.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may indicate systemic quality issues that could deter potential residents.
- The negative impact of population and income metrics suggests that while there is investment potential, demand may not be sustainable in the long term.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, underlying risks related to population and income levels warrant careful consideration.
