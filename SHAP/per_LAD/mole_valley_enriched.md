# Mole Valley Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Mole Valley Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and the demographic of the population aged 65 and over. However, negative impacts arise from declining house price growth and a significant portion of care homes rated as "Good" and "Inadequate."

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates potential for improvement and investment opportunities.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a larger elderly population suggests increased demand for care services.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↓ Major driver; negative growth in house prices may deter investment attractiveness.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a significant number of homes rated as "Good" may indicate limited opportunities for improvement.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable income per head suggests better affordability for care services.
- **Total = Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing population can lead to increased demand for care facilities.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita indicates a well-established care infrastructure.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a notable percentage of inadequate ratings raises concerns about quality and investment risk.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may indicate underlying quality issues that could affect long-term viability.
- The negative house price growth could impact the financial stability of potential residents, affecting demand for care services.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are opportunities for investment, the quality concerns and housing market signals warrant careful consideration.
