# Mid Devon Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Mid Devon Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of residents aged 65 and over. However, the presence of care quality ratings that are less than satisfactory, such as "CQC Outstanding" and "CQC Inadequate," negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy and potential for increased investment returns.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of older residents suggests a growing demand for care services, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Negative impact; a low percentage of outstanding care quality ratings raises concerns about service quality, detracting from the investment appeal.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Secondary driver; a moderate percentage indicates some facilities are improving, which could positively influence future care standards.
- **Total=Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a larger population base can support demand for care services, contributing positively to the investment score.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Negative impact; a presence of inadequate ratings signals potential risks in care quality, which could deter investment.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which is favorable for investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Minor negative impact; while some homes are rated good, the overall quality perception is still affected by the lower ratings.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of both outstanding and inadequate care ratings creates a mixed quality perception, which could lead to uncertainty in investment outcomes.
- High demand for care services due to an aging population may not be met if care quality does not improve, potentially affecting long-term viability.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are strong indicators for investment, the quality of care services presents significant risks that need to be monitored closely.
