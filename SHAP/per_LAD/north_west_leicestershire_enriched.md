# Executive Brief
The investment score for North West Leicestershire Enriched is positively influenced by several key factors, primarily the percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and house price growth. However, the presence of care homes rated as "Inadequate" and the number of care homes per 10,000 population negatively impacts the score.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may signal systemic issues in care quality that could deter potential investors.
- The negative impact of "Inadequate" ratings and the low number of care homes per capita could indicate challenges in meeting demand for quality care.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns and accessibility issues warrant careful consideration before investment.
