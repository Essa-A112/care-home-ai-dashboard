# East Riding Of Yorkshire Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the East Riding of Yorkshire Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by several key factors, particularly the percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and house price growth. However, the presence of homes rated as "Inadequate" and "Outstanding" introduces some complexity to the overall assessment.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant number of care homes needing improvement, which may suggest potential for investment but also raises quality concerns.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices indicates a healthy local economy, which can attract more residents and investment.
- **Percent = 65 plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as "Good" reflects a level of quality that can enhance the investment appeal.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of homes rated "Outstanding" is less significant compared to those rated "Requires Improvement," indicating mixed quality.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; homes rated "Inadequate" pose a risk to the investment score, reflecting potential quality issues.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a declining or stagnant population could limit future demand for care services.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which can be attractive for investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may indicate underlying quality issues that could affect long-term sustainability.
- The presence of homes rated "Inadequate" could deter potential residents and impact overall demand for care services.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns highlighted by the CQC ratings necessitate careful consideration before investment.
