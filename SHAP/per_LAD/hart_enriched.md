# Hart Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Hart Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by the presence of CQC ratings indicating poor quality care, specifically the high percentage of care homes rated as "Outstanding" and "Requires Improvement." Conversely, the score is positively influenced by the percentage of care homes rated as "Good" and the growth in gross disposable household income per head.

- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ pushes score up; a significant proportion of homes rated as good enhances the attractiveness of the investment (major driver).

- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ pushes score up; higher gross disposable household income per head indicates stronger economic conditions, supporting investment viability (secondary driver).
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ pushes score down; a notable percentage of inadequate ratings signals poor care quality, further diminishing the investment score (secondary driver).
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ pushes score up; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services, positively influencing investment potential (secondary driver).
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ pushes score down; a higher number of care homes per 10,000 people may suggest oversupply, which can negatively impact profitability (secondary driver).

# Risks or Caveats
- The presence of a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" raises concerns about care quality, which could deter potential investors despite some positive economic indicators.
- The negative impact of house price growth may indicate broader economic challenges that could affect the sustainability of care home investments.

# Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the significant quality concerns in care ratings warrant a careful evaluation before investment.
