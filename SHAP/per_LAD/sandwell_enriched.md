# SHAP Analysis for Sandwell Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Sandwell Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and strong house price growth. However, the presence of a significant proportion of care homes rated as "Good" and a notable elderly population negatively impacts the score.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates potential for improvement and investment opportunities.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth suggests a robust property market, enhancing investment appeal.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a significant number of homes rated "Good" may indicate limited opportunities for improvement.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↓ Secondary driver; a higher elderly population could increase demand but may also indicate potential strain on care services.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income may limit the ability of residents to afford care services.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may imply higher demand but could also strain existing resources.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding homes can enhance the overall reputation of the area.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a presence of inadequate homes raises concerns about quality and care standards.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated "Requires Improvement" suggests potential for growth but also indicates existing quality issues.
- The balance between a growing elderly population and the quality of care services could lead to increased demand without adequate supply.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are promising investment opportunities, quality concerns and demographic pressures warrant careful consideration.
