# Redbridge Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Redbridge Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). While house price growth is a positive factor, the overall quality ratings of care homes present significant concerns.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ pushes score down; this is a major driver indicating a significant portion of care homes do not meet quality standards.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ pushes score up; this is a strong positive factor reflecting increasing property values, which can attract investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ pushes score down; this secondary driver shows that a notable percentage of care homes are rated as "Good," but not sufficient to offset the negatives.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ pushes score down; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over may indicate increased demand for care services, but also raises concerns about service capacity.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ pushes score down; this factor indicates a small but concerning number of care homes rated as "Inadequate," further detracting from the investment appeal.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ pushes score down; a larger population may suggest demand, but it also raises questions about the adequacy of care services available.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ pushes score up; this minor positive driver indicates a small percentage of care homes that are rated "Outstanding," which is a positive sign for quality.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ pushes score up; this indicates a slight positive economic factor, suggesting residents have a reasonable income level to support care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" poses a significant risk to the overall investment attractiveness despite positive house price growth.
- The demographic trend of an increasing elderly population may lead to higher demand but could also strain existing care services if quality does not improve.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality of care homes presents significant risks that need to be addressed.
