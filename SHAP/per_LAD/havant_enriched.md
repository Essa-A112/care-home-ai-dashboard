# SHAP Analysis for Havant Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Havant Enriched is positively influenced by several key factors, particularly the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and strong house price growth. However, the presence of homes rated as "Inadequate" and "Good" negatively impacts the overall score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates significant room for improvement in care quality, which may attract investment for upgrades.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated as outstanding contributes positively, reflecting some high-quality care options.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate ratings detracts from the investment score, highlighting quality concerns.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests residents have more financial capacity, potentially supporting care home investments.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per 10,000 people indicates a competitive market, which can be attractive for investors.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated as good negatively impacts the score, suggesting a lack of quality assurance in the majority of care services.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may indicate higher demand but could also strain existing resources if care quality is lacking.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may signal potential challenges in meeting care standards, which could deter some investors.
- The presence of homes rated as "Inadequate" alongside strong economic indicators suggests a disparity in care quality that could affect long-term sustainability.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators for investment, the mixed quality of care services presents potential risks that need to be addressed.
