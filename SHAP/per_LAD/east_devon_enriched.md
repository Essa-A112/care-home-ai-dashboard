# SHAP Analysis for East Devon Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for East Devon Enriched is positively influenced primarily by house price growth, while the presence of care quality issues, particularly in the "Requires Improvement" and "Outstanding" categories, negatively impacts the score. This duality suggests a strong housing market but raises concerns about care home quality.

## Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; significant growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy and potential for investment returns.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement signals quality concerns, which detracts from the investment score.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a growing elderly population suggests increasing demand for care services, positively impacting investment potential.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Secondary driver; a low percentage of outstanding care homes indicates limited high-quality options, negatively affecting the score.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ Minor driver; a reasonable proportion of good-rated care homes supports a positive investment environment.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes raises red flags regarding service quality.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which is beneficial for investment.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a declining total population could indicate reduced demand for care services, negatively impacting the score.

## Risks or Caveats
- The strong house price growth may not be sustainable if care quality issues persist, potentially leading to a decline in investment attractiveness.
- The balance between an aging population and the quality of care services may create a mismatch in supply and demand.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while house price growth is promising, significant care quality concerns must be addressed to ensure sustainable investment.
