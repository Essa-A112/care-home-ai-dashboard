# SHAP Analysis for Darlington Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Darlington Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by poor house price growth and a significant proportion of care homes rated as requiring improvement. While there are some positive indicators, such as a growing elderly population, the overall quality of care facilities presents a concern for potential investors.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↓ (major driver) - A significant negative impact on the score, indicating stagnant or declining property values.
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ (major driver) - A high percentage of care homes needing improvement suggests quality issues, deterring investment.
- **Percent = 65 plus**: ↑ (secondary driver) - A growing elderly population indicates increasing demand for care services, which could positively influence investment.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ (secondary driver) - While still a concern, the presence of inadequate ratings highlights areas needing urgent attention, which could lead to future improvements.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ (minor driver) - A moderate number of care homes rated as good provides some reassurance about quality.
- **Total = Population**: ↑ (minor driver) - A stable population size suggests a consistent demand for care services.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ (minor driver) - The number of care homes relative to the population indicates availability, but does not directly correlate with quality.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↓ (minor driver) - A low percentage of outstanding ratings reflects a lack of top-tier care facilities, negatively impacting the overall score.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement may overshadow the potential benefits of a growing elderly population.
- The presence of inadequate ratings raises concerns about immediate care quality, which could affect long-term investment viability.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are growth indicators, significant quality issues in care homes present substantial risks.
