# Tamworth Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Tamworth Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and the proportion of care homes requiring improvement. However, the presence of care homes rated as good and inadequate negatively impacts the score, indicating mixed quality in care provision.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement indicates potential for growth but raises concerns about current service quality.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable proportion of care homes rated as good detracts from the score, suggesting quality issues in the overall care landscape.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower number of care homes per capita may indicate limited availability of services, negatively impacting the score.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↓ Secondary driver; lower gross disposable household income per head suggests reduced financial capacity for care services, affecting investment potential.
- **Total Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing population can indicate increased demand for care services, positively influencing the score.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes contributes positively, reflecting pockets of high-quality care.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes negatively impacts the overall perception of care quality in the area.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may signal systemic issues in care quality, which could deter potential investors.
- The mixed ratings for care homes (good vs. inadequate) suggest variability in service quality, which could affect overall demand and investment stability.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators, the quality of care services presents significant risks that must be addressed.
