# Staffordshire Moorlands Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Staffordshire Moorlands Enriched is positively influenced by several factors, particularly the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). However, the presence of care homes per capita and gross disposable household income (GDHI) per head are negatively impacting the score.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant number of homes needing improvement, which may suggest potential for investment.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a substantial proportion of homes rated as "Good" reflects a generally positive quality of care, enhancing investment appeal.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices indicates a potentially lucrative market for care home investments.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; while a small percentage indicates some homes are rated inadequate, it highlights areas needing urgent attention.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding homes adds to the overall quality perception, albeit minimally.
- **Total = Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a larger population can indicate a broader market for care services.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↓ Negative driver; a lower number of homes per capita suggests potential supply issues, which could limit investment attractiveness.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↓ Negative driver; lower disposable income per head may restrict the ability of residents to afford care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" could indicate systemic quality issues that may deter potential residents.
- The negative impact of care homes per capita and GDHI suggests that while demand may be present, financial viability could be a concern.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators for investment, the quality concerns and economic factors warrant careful consideration.
