# North Norfolk Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the North Norfolk Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by several key factors, particularly the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and strong house price growth. These elements suggest a growing demand for care services, although the quality ratings indicate potential challenges.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates significant room for improvement in care quality, which may attract investment to enhance services.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy, potentially increasing demand for care services.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as "Good" reflects a level of quality that can positively influence investment decisions.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates a growing market for care services.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; a positive gross disposable household income per head suggests residents have more financial capacity to pay for care services.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita may indicate a competitive market, which can influence investment attractiveness.
- **Total=Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a larger population base can support a wider range of care services and facilities.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; while a smaller percentage, outstanding ratings can enhance the reputation of care homes, attracting more residents.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may signal underlying quality issues that could deter potential investors.
- While house price growth is strong, it may not directly correlate with the affordability of care services for the local population.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators, the quality ratings present significant risks that need to be addressed before making investment decisions.
