# SHAP Analysis for Epping Forest Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Epping Forest Enriched is positively influenced by high levels of care homes requiring improvement and strong house price growth. However, the presence of care homes rated as inadequate and good negatively impacts the overall score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a significant positive impact, indicating that many care homes in the area are currently rated as requiring improvement, which suggests potential for enhancement and investment.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: This is also a major driver, reflecting a strong increase in property values, which can attract more residents and investment into the area.
- **CQC Good (↓)**: This secondary driver negatively impacts the score, as a lower percentage of care homes rated as good suggests quality concerns that may deter investment.
- **GDHI per head 2022 (↑)**: This factor has a minor positive influence, indicating that the gross disposable household income per person is relatively high, which can support investment in care services.
- **CQC Outstanding (↑)**: This factor contributes positively, albeit to a lesser extent, indicating that there are some care homes performing exceptionally well, which can enhance the area's attractiveness.
- **CQC Inadequate (↓)**: This secondary driver negatively affects the score, as a presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about service quality.
- **Care Homes per 10k (↓)**: This factor has a minor negative impact, suggesting that the number of care homes relative to the population may not be sufficient to meet demand.
- **Percent 65+ (↑)**: This factor positively influences the score, indicating a growing elderly population that may increase demand for care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of care homes rated as inadequate alongside those requiring improvement may signal a risk to overall service quality, potentially affecting long-term investment viability.
- The mixed ratings from the CQC could lead to reputational risks, impacting investor confidence.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the mixed quality of care services presents potential risks that need to be monitored closely.
