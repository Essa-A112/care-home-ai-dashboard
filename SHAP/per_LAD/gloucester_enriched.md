# SHAP Analysis for Gloucester Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Gloucester Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). This significant drawback is compounded by the presence of homes rated as "Outstanding," "Inadequate," and "Good," which further detract from the overall score.

- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of homes in this category significantly lowers the investment score.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices indicates a potentially attractive investment environment.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of homes rated as "Outstanding" is limited, negatively affecting the score.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated as "Inadequate" adds to concerns about care quality.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; while some homes are rated "Good," their limited presence does not sufficiently boost the score.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head suggests economic constraints in the area.
- **Percent = 65 plus**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over may indicate increased demand for care, but also raises concerns about service quality.
- **Care = Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per 10,000 people may suggest better access to services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" poses a significant risk to investment viability, indicating potential quality issues.
- While house price growth is positive, it may not be sufficient to offset the negative impacts of care quality ratings.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" raises concerns about care quality, which could affect long-term investment stability.
