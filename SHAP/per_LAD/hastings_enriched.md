# SHAP Analysis for Hastings Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Hastings Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of care quality ratings requiring improvement. However, the presence of lower ratings for good and inadequate care quality also introduces some concerns.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust property market, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests potential for operational enhancements, which could lead to increased demand.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of homes rated as good negatively impacts the overall quality perception, which could deter investment.
- **Percent = 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a growing elderly population indicates increasing demand for care services, positively influencing investment potential.
- **Total = Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a stable or growing population supports the demand for care services, contributing positively to the investment score.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; a presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about quality, negatively affecting the investment outlook.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes adds a positive note, suggesting some high-quality options are available.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a reasonable number of care homes per capita indicates accessibility, which can support investment attractiveness.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may indicate systemic issues in quality, which could pose risks to long-term investment viability.
- The presence of inadequate care ratings could deter potential residents, impacting occupancy rates and revenue.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns necessitate a careful evaluation of potential risks before investment.
