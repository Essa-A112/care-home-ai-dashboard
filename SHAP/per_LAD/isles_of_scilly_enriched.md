# SHAP Analysis for Isles Of Scilly Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for the Isles Of Scilly Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by high ratings in CQC assessments and population growth. However, the presence of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" presents a significant concern that could impact future investment decisions.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a high positive impact, indicating that a significant portion of care homes are rated as needing improvement, which suggests potential for growth but also raises quality concerns.
- **CQC Good (↑)**: This is a secondary driver, reflecting a solid number of care homes rated as good, which supports a positive investment outlook.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: This factor positively influences the score, indicating a healthy increase in property values, which can attract investment.
- **Total Population (↑)**: A growing population is a positive indicator for demand in care services, contributing to a stronger investment case.
- **Percent 65plus (↑)**: The percentage of the population aged 65 and over is a minor driver, suggesting a growing market for elderly care services.
- **GDHI per head 2022 (↑)**: The gross disposable household income per head is a minor positive factor, indicating potential spending power in the area.
- **Care Homes per 10k (↑)**: This factor shows a slight positive influence, suggesting a reasonable availability of care homes relative to the population.
- **CQC Outstanding (↑)**: While this is a minor driver, it reflects a small number of care homes achieving the highest quality rating, enhancing the overall perception of care quality.
- **CQC Inadequate (↓)**: This is a negative driver, indicating that some care homes are rated as inadequate, which detracts from the overall investment score.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" may indicate potential quality issues that could deter investment despite other positive indicators.
- The balance between population growth and the quality of care services must be monitored closely to ensure sustainable investment.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns highlighted by inadequate ratings warrant careful consideration before investment.
