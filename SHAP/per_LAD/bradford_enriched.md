# Bradford Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for the Bradford Enriched Local Authority District is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and strong house price growth. However, the presence of a significant elderly population and lower gross disposable household income per head are factors that negatively impact the score.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a need for improvement in care quality, which can signal potential for investment in upgrades.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a healthy local economy and increased demand for care services.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the elderly population may indicate increased demand for care services but also raises concerns about resource strain.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as "Good" reflects positively on the quality of care, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Secondary driver; lower gross disposable household income per head suggests limited financial capacity among residents, which may affect care home viability.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated as "Inadequate" indicates some quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of homes rated as "Outstanding" highlights pockets of high-quality care, which can enhance overall attractiveness.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may imply greater demand but can also strain existing resources and services.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita may indicate saturation in the market, potentially limiting growth opportunities.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" could indicate systemic issues that may require significant investment to address.
- The combination of a growing elderly population and lower disposable income may create a challenging environment for care home sustainability.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns and economic factors warrant a careful evaluation before investment.
