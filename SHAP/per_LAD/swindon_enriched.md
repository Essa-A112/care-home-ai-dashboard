# SHAP Analysis for Swindon Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Swindon Enriched is positively influenced primarily by significant house price growth, which is a major driver of potential returns. However, the presence of multiple quality indicators from the Care Quality Commission (CQC) that are rated as requiring improvement or inadequate negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust property market, enhancing investment potential.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Significant negative impact; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests quality concerns that may deter investment.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ Minor negative impact; a low percentage of outstanding care homes indicates limited high-quality options available.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ Minor negative impact; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over may increase demand for care services but also raises concerns about capacity and quality.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor negative impact; the presence of inadequate care homes further diminishes the attractiveness of the investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Minor negative impact; while some homes are rated good, the overall quality perception is still affected by the other negative ratings.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor negative impact; a larger population may suggest demand but does not directly correlate with care quality.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor negative impact; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the ability of residents to afford care services.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate poses a risk to the sustainability of demand and could lead to reputational damage.
- While house price growth is strong, it may not be sustainable if the quality of care does not improve, potentially leading to a decline in investment attractiveness.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while house price growth is promising, the quality concerns highlighted by CQC ratings necessitate careful consideration before investment.
