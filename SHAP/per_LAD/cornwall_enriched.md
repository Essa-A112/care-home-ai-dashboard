# SHAP Analysis for Cornwall Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Cornwall Enriched is positively influenced by strong house price growth and a significant elderly population. However, the presence of care quality ratings that require improvement and a high percentage of care homes rated as inadequate negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a healthy property market, boosting investment potential.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Secondary driver; a significant proportion of care facilities rated as requiring improvement detracts from the investment appeal.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as good indicates quality concerns that may affect investment decisions.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population can imply greater demand but also increases competition for resources.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes enhances the overall quality perception, positively impacting the score.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which is favorable for investment.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; a percentage of inadequate ratings highlights quality issues that could deter investment.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; a decrease in gross disposable household income per head may limit the financial capacity of potential clients.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of care homes rated as requiring improvement and inadequate could signal potential operational challenges, impacting long-term investment viability.
- While house price growth is strong, the quality of care services may not meet the expectations of potential residents, creating a mismatch in demand and supply.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators, the quality concerns in care services warrant a thorough assessment before investment.
