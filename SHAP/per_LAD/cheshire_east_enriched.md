# Cheshire East Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for Cheshire East Enriched is positively influenced by significant house price growth and a high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement. However, the presence of a lower percentage of care homes rated as good negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices indicates a robust local economy, enhancing investment potential.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests potential for operational enhancements and investment opportunities.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a lower percentage of care homes rated as good detracts from the investment appeal, indicating quality concerns.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over suggests increased demand for care services.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; the presence of care homes rated as inadequate indicates areas needing urgent attention, which could present investment opportunities.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests residents have more financial capacity to afford care services.
- **Total Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a declining total population may indicate reduced demand for care services in the long term.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes reflects positively on the local care sector's potential.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement could signal systemic issues that may deter investment despite potential for growth.
- The negative impact of a lower percentage of good-rated care homes may overshadow some positive indicators, creating uncertainty in the investment landscape.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are strong growth indicators, quality concerns in care services warrant careful evaluation before investment.
