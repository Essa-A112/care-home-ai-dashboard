# South Hams Enriched — SHAP Interpretation

# Executive Brief
The investment score for South Hams Enriched is positively influenced by a significant percentage of care homes requiring improvement and notable house price growth. However, the presence of care homes rated as 'Good' and 'Inadequate' negatively impacts the overall score, indicating mixed quality in care services.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a high positive impact, indicating that a significant portion of care homes are currently rated as requiring improvement, which suggests potential for enhancement and investment.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: A strong positive factor, reflecting an upward trend in property values, which can attract more residents and increase demand for care services.
- **Percent 65+ (↑)**: This secondary driver indicates a growing elderly population, suggesting an increasing demand for care services in the area.
- **CQC Good (↓)**: This factor negatively impacts the score, as a lower percentage of care homes rated as 'Good' suggests quality concerns that could deter investment.
- **Total Population (↑)**: A positive influence, indicating a stable or growing population base that supports demand for care services.
- **GDHI per head 2022 (↑)**: This factor positively contributes to the score, as higher disposable income per head can enhance the ability of residents to afford care services.
- **Care Homes per 10k (↑)**: This indicates a reasonable availability of care homes, which supports the overall investment attractiveness of the area.
- **CQC Outstanding (↑)**: A minor positive driver, reflecting a small percentage of care homes rated as 'Outstanding', which can enhance the area's reputation for quality care.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of care homes rated as 'Inadequate' raises concerns about the quality of care, which could negatively impact the area's attractiveness for investment despite other positive factors.
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests that while there is potential for growth, it also indicates existing quality issues that need addressing.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators for investment, the quality concerns highlighted by the CQC ratings warrant careful consideration.
