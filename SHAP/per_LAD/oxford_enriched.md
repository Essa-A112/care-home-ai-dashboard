# SHAP Analysis for Oxford Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for the Oxford Enriched Local Authority District is negatively impacted primarily due to poor CQC ratings and declining house price growth. Key factors such as a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and an increasing elderly population further contribute to the downward trend.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ pushes score down; a major driver indicating a significant number of care homes are not meeting required standards.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ pushes score down; a major driver reflecting declining property values, which can deter investment.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ pushes score down; a secondary driver showing a growing elderly population, which may increase demand but also highlights potential strain on care services.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ pushes score down; a secondary driver indicating that fewer care homes are rated as good, reflecting quality concerns.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ pushes score down; a minor driver suggesting that the availability of care homes is not sufficient to meet demand.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ pushes score down; a minor driver indicating a general population trend that may not support growth in care services.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ pushes score up; a minor positive driver indicating some care homes are rated outstanding, which is a positive signal for quality.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ pushes score down; a minor driver showing that some care homes are rated inadequate, further impacting overall quality perception.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↑ pushes score up; a minor positive driver indicating a higher gross disposable household income, which may support investment potential.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement alongside inadequate ratings raises concerns about the overall quality of care, which could deter potential investors.
- While the elderly population is increasing, the simultaneous decline in house price growth may limit financial viability for new care home developments.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the negative indicators related to care quality and property values suggest significant risks that need to be addressed before making investment decisions.
