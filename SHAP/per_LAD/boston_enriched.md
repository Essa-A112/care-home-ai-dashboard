# SHAP Analysis for Boston Enriched Local Authority District

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Boston Enriched is positively influenced primarily by strong house price growth, which is a major driver of potential returns. However, the presence of care quality ratings that require improvement and are rated inadequate negatively impacts the score, indicating potential risks in care home investments.

## Drivers
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; significant growth in house prices suggests a robust market, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable proportion of care homes requiring improvement detracts from the overall quality perception.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate care homes raises concerns about care quality, negatively impacting investment confidence.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; while some homes are rated good, this is outweighed by the negative ratings, suggesting mixed quality.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates increased demand for care services, supporting investment potential.
- **Total=Population**: ↑ Minor driver; a growing population can lead to higher demand for care homes, positively influencing the investment score.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding care homes adds some positive sentiment to the overall quality landscape.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower gross disposable household income per head may limit the ability of residents to afford care services, posing a risk to investment returns.

## Risks or Caveats
- The presence of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate may deter potential residents, impacting occupancy rates and revenue.
- While house price growth is strong, it may not fully compensate for the risks associated with care quality ratings.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the quality of care homes presents significant risks that must be monitored closely.
