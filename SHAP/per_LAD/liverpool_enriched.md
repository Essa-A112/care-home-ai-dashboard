# SHAP Analysis for Liverpool Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Liverpool Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). However, the presence of a significant elderly population and lower Gross Disposable Household Income (GDHI) per head negatively impacts the score.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ Major driver; a high percentage indicates a significant number of homes needing improvement, suggesting potential for investment.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↑ Secondary driver; a notable portion of homes rated as "Good" reflects a stable quality of care, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Secondary driver; positive growth in house prices suggests a healthy local economy, which can support care home demand.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↑ Minor driver; while a smaller percentage, the presence of inadequate ratings signals areas needing urgent attention, which could deter investment.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↓ Minor driver; a higher percentage of elderly residents may indicate increased demand for care services but also raises concerns about resource strain.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings adds to the overall quality perception, positively influencing the score.
- **GDHI=per head 2022**: ↓ Minor driver; lower disposable income per head suggests potential financial constraints for residents, which could limit care home affordability.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ Minor driver; a lower ratio of care homes may indicate limited availability, which could affect service access and investment viability.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of homes rated as "Requires Improvement" may indicate systemic issues within the care sector, posing a risk to potential investments.
- The significant elderly population could lead to increased demand for services, but it may also strain existing resources if not managed effectively.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are positive indicators, the challenges related to quality ratings and economic factors warrant careful consideration before investment.
