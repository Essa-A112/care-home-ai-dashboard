# Dorset Enriched SHAP Analysis

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Dorset Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by the high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). However, positive influences include significant house price growth and a notable percentage of the population aged 65 and over, which may indicate a growing demand for care services.

## Drivers
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" negatively affects the investment score.
- **House=Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth in house prices suggests a robust local economy and potential for increased demand for care services.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ Secondary driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates a growing market for care services.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ Minor driver; a percentage of care homes rated as "Good" is low, which detracts from the overall quality perception.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger total population can imply more demand, but its negative impact suggests potential oversupply or quality concerns.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of care homes rated as "Outstanding" provides a positive signal for quality.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of care homes rated as "Inadequate" further diminishes the quality perception.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; the number of care homes per 10,000 people indicates availability, which can be a positive factor for investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" raises concerns about quality and could deter potential investors.
- While house price growth is strong, it may not directly correlate with the quality of care services available, leading to potential mismatches in demand and supply.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the investment score is significantly affected by quality concerns, despite positive economic indicators.
