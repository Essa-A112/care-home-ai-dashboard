# SHAP Analysis for Solihull Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Solihull Enriched is negatively impacted primarily due to a high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Good" by the Care Quality Commission (CQC). Additionally, house price growth is also a significant detractor, indicating potential market challenges.

## Drivers
- **% = CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes in this category significantly lowers the investment score.
- **% = CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; while some homes are rated as good, their presence does not offset the negative impact of those requiring improvement.
- **House = Price Growth %**: ↓ Secondary driver; negative growth in house prices suggests a declining market, which adversely affects investment attractiveness.
- **Percent = 65plus**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher percentage of the population aged 65 and over indicates potential demand for care services.
- **% = CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate ratings further diminishes the overall quality perception of care homes.
- **% = CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small number of outstanding ratings can enhance the attractiveness of the care sector.
- **GDHI = per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income per head suggests better financial capacity among residents.
- **Total = Population**: ↓ Minor driver; a larger population may indicate more competition for care services, impacting investment negatively.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes rated as "Requires Improvement" and "Inadequate" presents a significant risk to the overall quality perception, which may deter potential investors.
- While there is some demand indicated by the aging population, it may not be sufficient to counterbalance the negative quality ratings.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the significant quality concerns in care homes could pose risks to investment returns.
