# SHAP Analysis for Wealden Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Wealden Enriched is positively influenced by strong house price growth and a significant elderly population. However, the presence of care quality ratings that require improvement and are rated as inadequate negatively impacts the overall score.

## Drivers
- **House Price Growth %**: ↑ Major driver; strong growth indicates a robust housing market, enhancing investment attractiveness.
- **% CQC Requires Improvement**: ↓ Major driver; a high percentage of care homes requiring improvement suggests quality concerns, detracting from the investment score.
- **Percent 65+**: ↑ Secondary driver; a larger elderly population indicates higher demand for care services, positively influencing the score.
- **% CQC Good**: ↓ Secondary driver; a notable percentage of care homes rated as good reflects quality issues, negatively impacting the score.
- **% CQC Inadequate**: ↓ Minor driver; the presence of inadequate ratings raises concerns about care quality, further decreasing the score.
- **GDHI per head 2022**: ↑ Minor driver; higher gross disposable household income suggests greater financial capacity for care services, positively affecting the score.
- **% CQC Outstanding**: ↑ Minor driver; a small percentage of outstanding ratings indicates some high-quality care options, contributing positively to the score.
- **Care Homes per 10k**: ↑ Minor driver; a higher number of care homes per capita suggests better access to services, which is beneficial for investment.

## Risks or Caveats
- The conflicting signals of high demand (due to an aging population) and poor care quality ratings may deter potential investors.
- The significant percentage of care homes rated as requiring improvement or inadequate could pose reputational risks.

## Action
Proceed with caution; while there are positive indicators, the quality of care remains a significant concern that could affect long-term investment viability.
