# SHAP Analysis for Salford Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Salford Enriched is positively influenced by the high percentage of care homes requiring improvement and significant house price growth. However, the presence of a notable percentage of care homes rated as 'Good' and a growing elderly population negatively impacts the score.

## Drivers
- **CQC Requires Improvement (↑)**: This is a major driver with a high positive impact, indicating that many care homes need enhancements, which may suggest opportunities for investment.
- **House Price Growth % (↑)**: A significant positive factor, reflecting a robust property market that can attract more residents and potential investors.
- **CQC Good (↓)**: This is a secondary driver negatively impacting the score, suggesting that while some homes are rated 'Good', the overall quality perception may not be strong enough to boost investment confidence.
- **Percentage of Population Aged 65+ (↓)**: This factor negatively affects the score, indicating a demographic trend that may increase demand for care services but also raises concerns about capacity and quality.
- **Care Homes per 10k (↓)**: A negative driver, suggesting that the availability of care homes may not meet the needs of the population, potentially leading to overcrowding or lower quality.
- **Total Population (↓)**: This factor negatively influences the score, indicating that a declining or stagnant population could limit the market for care services.
- **CQC Inadequate (↓)**: A minor negative driver, reflecting that some homes are rated as inadequate, which can deter investment.
- **CQC Outstanding (↑)**: A small positive factor, indicating that a few homes are rated outstanding, which can enhance the overall perception of care quality in the area.

## Risks or Caveats
- The high percentage of care homes requiring improvement may indicate a need for significant investment to elevate standards, which could pose financial risks.
- The negative impact of the elderly population percentage suggests potential strain on resources, which could affect service quality and investor confidence.

## Action
Proceed with caution — while there are opportunities for investment, the quality concerns and demographic challenges warrant careful consideration.
