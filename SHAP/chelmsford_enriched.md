# SHAP Analysis for Chelmsford Enriched

## Executive Brief
The investment score for Chelmsford Enriched is negatively impacted primarily by declining house price growth and a high percentage of care quality ratings that are either inadequate or good. These factors suggest challenges in both property value appreciation and care home quality, which are critical for investment decisions.

- **House=Price Growth %**: ↓ (major driver) - A significant negative impact due to declining house price growth, indicating potential market instability.
- **%=CQC Good**: ↓ (secondary) - The presence of care homes rated as good is low, negatively affecting the investment score.
- **%=CQC Inadequate**: ↓ (secondary) - A notable percentage of care homes rated inadequate further detracts from the score, highlighting quality concerns.
- **%=CQC Requires Improvement**: ↑ (secondary) - A positive influence, indicating some care homes are on the path to improvement, which could enhance future scores.
- **Care=Homes per 10k**: ↓ (minor) - A lower number of care homes per capita suggests limited availability, negatively impacting the score.
- **Total=Population**: ↓ (minor) - A declining population may indicate reduced demand for care services, further lowering the investment score.
- **Percent=65plus**: ↑ (minor) - An increasing percentage of the population aged 65 and over could indicate a growing demand for care services, positively influencing the score.
- **%=CQC Outstanding**: ↓ (minor) - A low percentage of outstanding-rated care homes negatively impacts the overall quality perception.

## Risks or Caveats
- The combination of low house price growth and high percentages of inadequate care ratings presents a significant risk to investment viability.
- While there are some positive indicators (e.g., improvement ratings and aging population), they may not be sufficient to offset the negative drivers.

## Action
Proceed with caution — the negative indicators regarding house prices and care quality require careful consideration before making investment decisions.
