# High Peak — Zoning & Planning Summary (aggregated)

**Investment Brief: High Peak Care Home Opportunity**

**1. Executive Summary**  
The High Peak area presents a suitable opportunity for investment in C2 (care home/extra-care) facilities, driven by demographic trends and local policy alignment. The region is experiencing an increasing demand for adult social care services, supported by local planning frameworks that recognize the need for enhanced care facilities. Given the supportive zoning environment and the proactive stance of local authorities towards ageing populations, it is recommended to “Proceed” with this investment opportunity.

**2. Zoning & Planning Environment**  
High Peak has established permissions for C2 use, with explicit references to care-related allocations within its local planning documents. The flexibility of local zoning allows for the development of care homes and extra-care facilities, addressing the growing needs of the community. However, planning constraints such as flood risk areas and heritage considerations must be acknowledged, as they could impact specific development sites. There are no significant greenbelt restrictions noted that would impede the establishment of care facilities.

**3. Policy Signals & Strategic Fit**  
Local plans and regeneration strategies indicate a strong focus on addressing the needs of an ageing population. High Peak's growth targets align with the provision of housing and employment opportunities that intersect with healthcare needs. The strategic fit is reinforced by the local authority's commitment to enhancing adult social care services, which is a recurring theme across various planning documents.

**4. Infrastructure & Delivery Conditions**  
The area benefits from adequate connectivity and access, which are crucial for supporting new care home developments. Current infrastructure is generally ready to accommodate additional services; however, potential risks related to transport accessibility and the availability of essential services should be monitored. Ensuring that infrastructure can support increased demand will be vital for successful delivery.

**5. Demographic & Care Context**  
High Peak is experiencing significant demographic shifts, with an increasing proportion of the population aged 65 and over. This trend is coupled with health inequalities that necessitate a robust adult social care framework. The local authority is actively planning to address these pressures, indicating a forward-thinking approach to healthcare and social services.

**6. Investment Stance**  
This Local Authority District (LAD) is classified as a “Proceed” investment opportunity. The supportive policy environment, alignment with demographic trends, and readiness of local infrastructure present a favorable landscape for care home development. The evidence indicates low constraints and a clear demand for additional care facilities, making this a viable investment.
